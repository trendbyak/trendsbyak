Elastic & construction

Made to stretch. Made to be used.

A scrunchie's comfort and performance depend not only on the fabric, but also on the elastic, stitching and construction. We select the elastic according to the size and intended use of the scrunchie.

Flexible stretch Designed to provide comfortable stretch for everyday styling.
Good recovery Elastic is selected for practical stretch and recovery during use.
Secure construction The elastic is enclosed inside the fabric and securely stitched.
Size matched Elastic choice can vary according to scrunchie size and fabric.
Everyday friendly Suitable for repeated everyday styling when used appropriately.
Handmade finish Each piece is prepared and finished with attention to construction.

Important: Elastic performance can vary with fabric, size, frequency of use, stretching and storage. We do not claim a fixed number of stretches or a guaranteed lifetime. For large or specialised requirements, we can discuss the most suitable construction before production.

FAQs

Bulk order questions

What is the minimum bulk order?

The minimum bulk order is 50 pieces. For larger quantities, share your requirement so we can quote accordingly.

What fabrics are available?

Depending on availability, we can work with satin, organza, cotton, printed fabrics, georgette, crinkle and net options. Specific colours, prints and fabrics can be discussed for your order.

How durable is the elastic?

We use flexible elastic selected according to the scrunchie size, fabric and intended use. The elastic is enclosed and securely stitched. Performance depends on frequency of use, stretching, fabric and storage, so we do not promise a fixed lifetime.

Can I choose colours and fabrics?

Yes. Share your preferred colours, fabric and quantity. Availability can vary, especially for specific prints or large quantities.

Can I request a specific elastic feel?

Yes. You can tell us whether you prefer standard everyday stretch, extra stretch or a firmer hold. We can recommend the most suitable option based on the size and fabric.

Do you accept custom or event orders?

Yes. We can discuss requirements for weddings, return gifts, gifting, boutiques, brands and events.

How long does a bulk order take?

Production time depends on quantity, fabric availability and customisation. We will confirm the expected timeline before you place the order.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, Pan India delivery is available. Delivery charges and timelines depend on the destination and order size.
