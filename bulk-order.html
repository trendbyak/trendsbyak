What fabrics can I choose?

Depending on availability, we work with satin, organza, printed satin or cotton, plain cotton or satin, net, georgette, crinkled fabrics and other custom requirements.

Can I choose different colours?

Yes. We can discuss single-colour or multi-colour requirements depending on your quantity, fabric and collection.

Yes. We provide pan India delivery for bulk scrunchie orders.

Absolutely. Bulk scrunchies can be supplied for boutiques, retail stores, online brands and resale collections.

Custom branding and packaging can be discussed depending on the order quantity and requirements. Share your idea with us on WhatsApp.

WhatsApp us with your quantity, fabric, size, colour and delivery city. Bulk pricing depends on these details, so we'll provide an exact quotation.
