Cancellation & Refund Policy

Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be made as soon as possible after placing an order. We aim to accommodate cancellation requests received within 1 hour of placing the order, provided the order has not already been processed or dispatched.

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible and the applicable return policy will apply.

How to Cancel

Contact us through WhatsApp at 8050556004 or email aktrends4u@gmail.com with your order number and registered phone number/email.

Refund for Cancelled Orders

Where a cancellation is approved and a refund is due, we will initiate the refund through the applicable payment method. Bank/payment-provider processing times may affect when the amount is credited to your account.

Returns and Refunds

Approved returns are handled under our Returns & Exchanges Policy. Refunds are processed after the returned product is received and passes the applicable quality check.

Failed or Duplicate Payments

If your payment is debited but the order is not successfully created, or if you believe you have been charged more than once, contact us with the transaction details. We will verify the payment status and assist with the appropriate resolution.
