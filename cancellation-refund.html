Cancellation & Refund Policy

Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be made as soon as possible after placing an order. We aim to accommodate requests received within 1 hour, provided the order has not already been processed or dispatched.

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible and the applicable Returns & Exchanges Policy will apply.

How to Cancel

Contact us on WhatsApp/Phone at 8433998962 or email aktrends4u@gmail.com with your order number and registered contact details.

Refund for Cancelled Orders

Where an approved cancellation qualifies for a refund, we will initiate the refund through the applicable payment method. Bank or payment-provider processing times may affect when the amount is credited.

Returns and Refunds

Approved returns are handled under our Returns & Exchanges Policy. Refunds are processed after the returned product is received and passes the applicable quality check.

Failed or Duplicate Payments

If your payment is debited but an order is not successfully created, or if you believe you have been charged more than once, contact us with the transaction details. We will verify the payment status and assist with the appropriate resolution.
