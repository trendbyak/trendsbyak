Shipping Policy

Thank you for shopping with Trends by AK. We aim to process and dispatch every confirmed order as quickly as possible.

Order Processing

All confirmed orders are generally processed within 24–48 hours. Processing may take longer during sales, holidays, or periods of unusually high order volume.

Delivery Timelines

  • Metro cities: approximately 2–5 business days from dispatch.
  • Rest of India: approximately 3–7 business days from dispatch.

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary depending on the courier, destination, weather, local restrictions, or other circumstances beyond our control.

Shipping Charges

  • Prepaid orders above ₹1,499: Free shipping.
  • Cash on Delivery: ₹49 convenience fee, where COD is available.

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, tracking details may be shared through WhatsApp and/or email. If you do not receive a WhatsApp update, please check your registered email or contact us at 8050556004 or aktrends4u@gmail.com.

Split Shipments

Orders containing products with different availability may be shipped in multiple packages. In such cases, items may arrive separately.

Delays & Serviceability

Courier disruptions, weather conditions, local restrictions, incorrect or incomplete addresses, and remote-area serviceability can affect delivery times. We will assist you with available courier updates and alternate arrangements where possible.

Damaged or Tampered Packages

If your package appears visibly damaged or tampered with at delivery, please refuse delivery where possible and contact us promptly. For damaged or incorrect products, retain the packaging and product and contact us with photographs and order details.

Changes to this Policy

Trends by AK may update shipping charges, timelines, or serviceable locations from time to time. Any changes will apply prospectively and will be reflected on the website.
