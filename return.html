Returns & Refunds

At Trends by AK, we carefully check and pack every order. We want you to receive your products safely and as expected.

Important: We do not accept change-of-mind returns. Products can be considered for a return/replacement only when the item is damaged, defective, or incorrect, and the issue is reported within 7 days of delivery with the required proof.

1. When can I raise a claim?

You may contact us if you receive:

  • A damaged or broken product.
  • A defective product.
  • The wrong product, design, colour, size, or item compared with your order.
  • A product with a missing stone, part, component, or included accessory.
  • A product that has clearly been damaged during transit.

Claims must be raised within 7 days of delivery.

2. Unboxing video is compulsory

For every damaged, defective, or incorrect-item claim, you must provide a continuous, uncut and unedited unboxing video.

The video must clearly show:

  • The outer parcel/package before opening.
  • The shipping label and tracking details.
  • The complete opening of the parcel.
  • The product(s) received and the issue clearly visible.

Claims without the required unboxing video and supporting proof will not be accepted.

3. How to raise a claim

  1. Contact Trends by AK on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery.
  2. Share your order number, clear photographs/videos, and the continuous unboxing video.
  3. Explain the issue clearly and tell us whether you prefer a replacement or refund if your claim is approved.
  4. Our team will investigate the claim and communicate the decision within 3 days.
  5. If approved, we will arrange the applicable return/replacement process. For eligible wrong or damaged items, Trends by AK will bear the return shipping cost.
  6. If a refund is approved, it will be processed within 5–7 working days after approval.

4. Refund or replacement

For an approved eligible claim, the customer may choose between a replacement or refund, subject to product availability and the circumstances of the case.

Where a replacement is not available, a refund or store credit may be offered as applicable.

5. Jewellery

Jewellery is non-returnable and non-exchangeable once delivered, except when the item is received damaged, defective, or incorrect and the claim meets all requirements in this policy.

6. Hair accessories, scrunchies & fabric products

Scrunchies, bows, bonnets, headbands and other hair/fabric accessories are non-returnable and non-exchangeable once delivered, except when the item is received damaged, defective, or incorrect and the claim meets all requirements in this policy.

7. Customised, personalised & bulk orders

Customised, personalised and bulk orders are non-returnable and non-refundable. These orders also cannot be cancelled once the order is confirmed.

8. Change of mind, wrong size or unwanted items

We do not accept returns or exchanges because you changed your mind, do not like the product after receiving it, ordered the wrong size/design, or no longer require the item.

Return shipping is therefore not applicable for change-of-mind requests.

9. Order cancellation

Orders may be cancelled only before dispatch. Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled.

Customised, personalised and bulk orders cannot be cancelled once confirmed.

10. COD refunds

If a refund is approved for a COD order, the customer may choose to receive it through UPI, bank account, or store credit. We may request the details required to process the selected refund method.

11. Delivery refusal and RTO

If a customer refuses delivery, does not accept the parcel, or the parcel is returned to us as an RTO for reasons attributable to the customer, applicable shipping/RTO charges may be deducted from any eligible refund.

12. Parcel marked delivered but not received

If your tracking shows that the parcel was delivered but you have not received it, contact us on WhatsApp within 2 days of the delivery status so we can investigate the delivery issue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I return jewellery?

No. Jewellery is non-returnable and non-exchangeable after delivery, except for an approved damaged, defective, or incorrect-item claim supported by the required proof.

What if I receive a damaged product?

Contact us on WhatsApp within 7 days and share your order details, photographs/videos and the required continuous, uncut and unedited unboxing video. We will investigate and communicate the decision within 3 days.

What if I receive the wrong product?

Contact us on WhatsApp within 7 days with your order details and the required unboxing video and supporting proof. If approved, you can choose a replacement or refund, subject to the applicable process.

Is an unboxing video compulsory?

Yes. For damaged, defective, or incorrect-item claims, a continuous, uncut and unedited video showing the outer parcel, shipping label and complete opening of the parcel is compulsory. Claims without the required proof will not be accepted.

Can I exchange an item?

We do not offer exchanges for change of mind, wrong size, or preference. A replacement may be arranged for an approved damaged, defective, or incorrect-item claim.

Can I return a personalised or customised item?

No. Customised and personalised orders are non-returnable and non-refundable.

Can I return a bulk order?

No. Bulk orders are non-returnable and non-refundable.

How long does a refund take?

We investigate eligible claims and communicate the decision within 3 days. Once a refund is approved, it is processed within 5–7 working days.

What happens if my parcel is damaged?

Please show the parcel condition in your continuous unboxing video and contact us within 7 days. Share clear photographs/videos so we can investigate the issue.

Can I cancel after placing an order?

You can request cancellation only before dispatch. Customised, personalised and bulk orders cannot be cancelled once confirmed.

What if I ordered the wrong size?

Wrong-size orders are not eligible for return or exchange. Please check the product details carefully before placing your order.

What if I don't like the product after receiving it?

Change-of-mind returns are not accepted. We recommend reviewing the product information, images and details before ordering.

What if the parcel says delivered but I haven't received it?

Contact us on WhatsApp within 2 days of the delivery status so we can investigate with the delivery partner.

Who pays return shipping for an approved damaged or wrong-item claim?

For an approved claim where Trends by AK sent a wrong item or the product was damaged/defective before delivery, Trends by AK will bear the applicable return shipping cost.

Can I get a refund for a COD order?

Yes, if your claim is approved. You can choose UPI, bank account, or store credit for the refund.
