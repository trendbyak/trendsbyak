Excel column guide
Products: id, name, sku, price, sale_price, category, stock, description, image_url, collection, status, has_variants, cost_price, packaging_cost, shipping_cost, other_expense.

Variants: variant_id, product_id, product_sku, product_name, size, color, price, sale_price, stock, sku, image_url, status, cost_price, packaging_cost, shipping_cost, other_expense.

Profit: cost_price is COGS per unit. Packaging, shipping and other_expense are per-unit product costs. Business expenses are entered separately in Expenses.
