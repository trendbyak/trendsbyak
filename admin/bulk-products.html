Excel column guide
Products: id, name, sku, price, sale_price, category, stock, description, image_url, collection, status, has_variants.

Variants: variant_id, product_id, product_sku, product_name, size, color, price, sale_price, stock, sku, image_url, status.

For new variants, enter the parent product SKU (or ID), then size/color/price/stock. You may leave the parent fields blank only when continuing a group of variants directly below another variant row for the same product.
