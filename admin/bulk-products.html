Excel column guide
Products: id, name, sku, price, sale_price, category, stock, description, image_url, collection, status, has_variants.

Variants: variant_id, product_id, product_sku, size, color, price, sale_price, stock, sku, image_url, status.

One variant row = one selectable size/color combination. A product becomes a variant product when it has one or more variant rows.
