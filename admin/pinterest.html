📌 Pinterest Integration

Claim trendsbyak.in, prepare Pinterest tracking and keep the integration details in one place.

1. Website claim

HTML tag ready

The Pinterest verification tag from your Pinterest screen has been added to the customer-facing site through the global header.

<meta name="p:domain_verify" content="54f17140de35b2e569cb4b731230e86" />
Once the latest website deployment is live, return to Pinterest and click Claim your website.

2. What to do in Pinterest

  1. Keep Add HTML tag selected.
  2. Make sure the website URL is https://trendsbyak.in.
  3. Click Claim your website.
  4. Wait for Pinterest to verify the domain.
If Pinterest says it cannot find the tag, wait for the website deployment/cache to update and try again. Do not remove the tag.

3. Pinterest business setup

After the website is claimed, the next build step is Pinterest conversion tracking and product/catalog feeds.

  • Connect the Trends by AK Pinterest Business account.
  • Install the Pinterest Tag for page visits and conversions.
  • Track product views, add-to-cart and checkout/purchase events.
  • Prepare a product feed so jewellery, scrunchies and other products can become Pinterest product Pins.
