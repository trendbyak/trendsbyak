Returns & Exchanges

At Trends by AK, we want you to be happy with your purchase. Eligible unused and unworn products may be returned or exchanged subject to this policy.

Return Window

Eligible products may be requested for return or exchange within 5 days of delivery.

Eligibility

  • Items must be unused and unworn.
  • Original tags, packaging and invoice should be retained where applicable.
  • Items must not be damaged, altered, washed or used.
  • Items showing signs of use may be rejected after quality inspection.

Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, contact us promptly with your order number and clear photographs. We may request photographs of the package and an unboxing video where appropriate.

How to Request a Return or Exchange

Contact us on WhatsApp/Phone at 8433998962 or email aktrends4u@gmail.com. Please provide your order number and registered phone number or email address.

Approval & Pickup

Requests are reviewed for eligibility. If approved, we will provide pickup or return instructions. Returned products are inspected before a refund or exchange is finalized.

Refunds

For approved returns, eligible refunds are generally processed within 15 days after the returned item is received and passes quality inspection. The applicable refund method may depend on the original order and payment method.

Exchanges

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If the requested replacement is unavailable, we will contact you regarding the available resolution.

Non-returnable Situations

Products outside the return window, used or worn products, products missing required tags or packaging, and products damaged through customer use may not qualify for return or exchange.
