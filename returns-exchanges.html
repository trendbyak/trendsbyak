Returns & Exchanges

At Trends by AK, we want you to be happy with your purchase. Eligible unused and unworn products may be returned or exchanged subject to the conditions below.

Return Window

Eligible items may be requested for return or exchange within 5 days of delivery.

Eligibility

  • Products must be unused and unworn.
  • Original tags, packaging, and invoice should be retained where applicable.
  • Products must not be damaged, altered, washed, or used.
  • Items showing signs of use may be rejected after quality inspection.

Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, contact us as soon as possible with your order number and clear photographs. Where appropriate, we may also request an unboxing video and photographs of the outer package.

How to Request a Return or Exchange

Contact us through WhatsApp at 8050556004 or email aktrends4u@gmail.com. Please provide your order number and registered phone number or email address.

Approval & Pickup

Return requests are reviewed for eligibility. If approved, we will provide the next steps for pickup or return shipment. The product will be inspected after receipt before a refund or exchange is finalized.

Refunds

For approved returns, eligible refund amounts are generally processed within 15 days after the return is approved/received and passes quality inspection. Refund method may depend on the order and applicable payment/refund rules.

Exchange

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If the requested replacement is unavailable, we will discuss the available resolution with you.

Non-returnable Situations

Products outside the return window, used or worn items, products without required tags/packaging, and items damaged after delivery through customer use may not qualify for return or exchange.
