Privacy Policy

Trends by AK respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can contact us about your information.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, billing and delivery address, account information, order details, communication preferences, and information needed to process and support your purchases.

When you browse our website, technical information such as browser/device information, approximate location, IP address, pages visited, and cookies may also be processed for website operation, security, analytics, and improvement.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your customer account.
  • To process payments and fulfil orders.
  • To arrange delivery and provide tracking updates.
  • To send order confirmations and important service communications.
  • To respond to customer support requests.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • To improve our website, products, and customer experience.
  • To send promotional communications where permitted and according to your preferences.

Payments

Payments are processed through our payment service providers. Trends by AK does not intentionally store complete card, UPI, or banking credentials on the website. Payment providers may process information according to their own privacy and security policies.

Cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, maintain shopping functionality, understand website usage, and improve performance.

Sharing Information

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers involved in payment processing, delivery, website hosting, authentication, customer communication, analytics, and technical support. We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

Communications

Transactional communications such as order confirmations, delivery updates, account messages, and security-related emails may be sent when necessary to provide our services. Promotional communications can be opted out of where applicable.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer information. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so customers should also protect their passwords and account access.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and transaction records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and protect our rights.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held about you. You may also contact us regarding communication preferences or privacy concerns.

Children

Our website is intended for general consumers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for independent account creation or purchases.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy when our services, practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.
